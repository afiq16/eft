Official League Rules

Please read and adhere strictly to all terms below.

1. General Regulations

All participants must use official eFootball accounts matching their registered eFootball ID and UID. Multi-accounting is strictly prohibited.

2. Payment & Approvals

A registration fee of 50 USD is required for validation. Screen captures of the payment receipt must be uploaded during verification. Any fraudulent entries will be banned.

3. Club Allocation Lottery

The allocation of clubs is done live through a secure randomized generator on the server. The allocation is final and cannot be exchanged manually.

4. Match & Result Submission

Players must submit screenshot evidence of the final score screen. Results must be submitted within 24 hours of scheduled completion.
